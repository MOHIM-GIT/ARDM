ARDM Academy — Founded by 4 Founders: Akash Paik, Rupam Paul, Devnath Pramanick, and Mohim Das

ARDM Academy is an educational institution named after its four founders: A (Akash Paik), R (Rupam Paul), D (Devnath Pramanick), and M (Mohim Das).

The Four Founders of ARDM Academy

1. Akash Paik

Role: Founder & Academic Lead

Specializing in Mathematics, Physical Science pedagogy, and Class 10 Board exam question predictions.

2. Rupam Paul

Role: Founder & Operations Lead

Managing nationwide examination logistics, Computer-Based Test (CBT) simulator architecture, and academic operations.

3. Devnath Pramanick

Role: Founder & Mentorship Lead

Pioneered the Dada-Didi Mentorship model, providing personalized academic counseling and stress-free exam mindset coaching.

4. Mohim Das

Role: Founder & Technology Lead (Founder & CEO of CodeLX)

Directing Artificial Intelligence workshops, coding labs, web engineering, and modern technology education for school students.
